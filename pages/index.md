# Ashish Mahabal

Deputy Director, Center for Data Driven Discovery, Caltech

I work on finding and classifying things that change in the sky, using machine
learning on data from large sky surveys. I lead the machine learning for the
[Zwicky Transient Facility](/research/#ztf) and for the RAPID pipeline of the
[Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope](/research/#roman). The same methods carry
over to other fields with complex data: I work with the Data Science group at
JPL on early detection of cancer ([EDRN](/research/#edrn)), on biomarker
knowledge bases, and on predicting fungal contaminants for space missions.

PhD in Astronomy, IUCAA, Pune (1998).
